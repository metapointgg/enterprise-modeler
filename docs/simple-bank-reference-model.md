# Simple Bank Reference Model

This seed demonstrates Enterprise Modeler using an illustrative UK retail bank offering savings and consumer lending.

## Scope

The model includes 93 processes:
- 1 L0 operating model
- 12 L1 end-to-end process domains
- 80 L2 banking and support processes

It also includes:
- 45 sample people and 45 positions
- 22 systems
- 18 application interfaces
- 35 data objects
- 20 data flows
- 16 regulatory sources
- 12 business rules
- 20 controls
- 18 risks
- 14 regulatory requirements
- 12 policies / standards
- 1120 explicit enterprise relationships, in addition to relationships derived automatically from structured catalogue fields

## Core systems

- Salesforce CRM
- Temenos Transact (T24) for savings and lending
- IRIS Financials
- Microsoft SharePoint Online for document management
- LSEG World-Check One as an illustrative sanctions / PEP screening service
- An illustrative digital identity verification service
- Experian as an illustrative credit bureau
- UK payments gateway
- AML and fraud monitoring platforms
- Data warehouse and Power BI
- Microsoft Entra ID and Sentinel
- ServiceNow
- Treasury / ALM tooling

## Important note

This is a reference architecture and operating model, not legal, regulatory or implementation advice. Regulatory names and control descriptions are illustrative and should be validated for the specific legal entity, permissions, products, customer base and jurisdictions before operational use.
